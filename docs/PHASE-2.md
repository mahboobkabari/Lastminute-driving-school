# Phase 2: UI/UX Design

Status: Complete

## Design direction

Lastminute Driving School uses a premium, mobile-first service design built around deep navy, red and white. The interface intentionally avoids generic stock-template styling. It uses strong typography, restrained motion, generous whitespace, real learner photography and short conversion paths.

## Research inputs

- GOV.UK practical driving test guidance was used to shape the test-preparation content. The current car test includes an eyesight check, vehicle safety questions, general driving ability including independent driving, and a reversing exercise.
- Local market research shows nearby driving schools compete heavily on clear pricing, experience, reviews, service area coverage and direct booking/contact paths.
- The supplied Google Business Profile data establishes 5.0 stars and 82 reviews for Lastminute Driving School at Bravington Rd, W9 3AP.
- Supplied Google review material strongly supports a brand voice centred on patience, clarity, confidence, practical feedback and test preparation.

## UI principles

1. Mobile-first and phone-friendly.
2. One dominant CTA: Book a Lesson.
3. Secondary CTA: View Courses.
4. Reviews and trust proof appear early.
5. Real learner imagery is prioritised over generic stock imagery.
6. Cards use medium radii and restrained shadows.
7. Motion is limited to small hover/transition effects.
8. Accessibility and semantic HTML remain part of implementation, not a final-stage patch.

## Homepage prototype

The coded homepage in `src/app/page.tsx` is the Phase 2 visual prototype. It demonstrates the intended hero, trust bar, positioning, courses, test-preparation section, proof, reviews and CTA system.
