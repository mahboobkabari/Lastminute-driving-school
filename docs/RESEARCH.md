## 2. DVSA & UK Driving Test Regulations (2026 Guidance)

### 2.1 The 2026 DVSA Practical Driving Test Structure
The UK car practical driving test is administered by the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA). It lasts approximately **38 to 40 minutes** and consists of 5 core assessment components:

1. **Eyesight Check:**
   * Candidates must read a vehicle number plate from a distance of **20.0 metres** (or 20.5 metres for old-style number plates) in good daylight.
   * Failure results in an immediate test cancellation and licence revocation.
2. **Vehicle Safety Questions ("Show Me, Tell Me"):**
   * **"Tell Me" Question (Before setting off):** The examiner asks 1 verbal explanation question regarding vehicle safety and maintenance (e.g. checking tyre pressure, tyre tread depth, brake fluid, engine coolant, headlights/taillights).
   * **"Show Me" Question (While driving on the move):** The examiner asks the candidate to operate a control while driving safely (e.g. washing and wiping the front or rear windscreen, operating the rear demister, sounding the horn, opening/closing a window).
3. **General Driving Ability:**
   * Driving through various road conditions, roundabouts, pedestrian crossings, speed transitions, traffic junctions, and urban congestion.
   * Assessment of observation, mirror routines (MSPSL - Mirrors, Signal, Position, Speed, Look), clearance to parked cars, and lane discipline.
4. **Reversing Exercise (One manoeuvre chosen by examiner):**
   * *Parallel park* at the side of the road (behind a target vehicle within 2 car lengths).
   * *Bay park* (either driving in and reversing out, or reversing in and driving out).
   * *Pull up on the right-hand side of the road*, reverse back for 2 car lengths, and rejoin traffic safely.
5. **Independent Driving (~20 minutes):**
   * Candidates drive independently for approximately 20 minutes without step-by-step turn guidance.
   * In 4 out of 5 tests (80%), candidates follow a **DVSA-provided Sat Nav** (TomTom device pre-loaded with the route).
   * In 1 out of 5 tests (20%), candidates follow **traffic signs** to a designated destination.
   * *Crucial Rule:* Taking a wrong turn does not constitute a driving fault as long as it is done safely.

6. **Controlled Emergency Stop:**
   * Carried out in approximately 1 out of 3 tests (33%) to assess quick, controlled braking and clutch discipline without skidding, followed by 360-degree observation before moving off.

### 2.2 2026 DVSA Driving Test Booking Rules & Policy Updates
The DVSA has implemented strict digital anti-tout and booking integrity measures:
* **Strict Candidate-Only Self-Booking:** Learners must book and manage practical tests directly through GOV.UK using their own driving licence number. Automated bots and third-party commercial "cancellation finder" resale exploits are restricted.
* **Maximum 2 Changes Limit:** A test booking can be modified a maximum of **2 times** (date, time, or location). After 2 changes, the candidate must cancel and start a new booking.
* **Geographical Proximity Rule:** Test rescheduling is restricted to the **3 nearest test centres** relative to the candidate’s home postcode or initial booking centre.
* **Vehicle Suitability for Test:** If using an instructor car or private car, it must have dual controls (recommended/standard for instructor cars), extra rear-view mirror for the examiner, fully functional seatbelts, L-plates, no warning lights on dashboard, and valid insurance covering driving tests.
